# Cabinet Directives

Cabinet Directives are federal policy instruments endorsed by Cabinet. They provide direction across government; they do not have the same legal status as statutes or regulations. This small, curated collection is tracked separately from the Treasury Board Policy Suite and policy implementation notices.

Status is one of **Announced**, **In Force**, **Superseded**, or **Retired**. Dates use `yyyy-mm-dd`; **Unknown** means the source does not establish that date. Publication dates use the source's `dcterms.issued` metadata where available. A page's last-modified date is not its effective date. Status and effective dates require source evidence and analyst review.

This document is the monitoring register. Add a linked level-two heading and the four fields below, or use the **New or Supercede Cabinet Directive** issue template. The shared Policy Watch job monitors these registered URLs. It does not discover new instruments. Tell the agent “cab directive change” with a URL to assess a new instrument, replacement or amendment using `skills/cabinet-directives/SKILL.md`. HTML, Markdown and screenshots are archived under readable instrument folders in `data/CabinetDirectives/`; first captures of older instruments are inventory baselines, not quarterly policy news. The collection is preliminary, not exhaustive.

## [Cabinet Directive on Getting Projects Built in Canada (2026)](https://www.canada.ca/en/one-canadian-economy/services/simplifying-canada-process/cabinet.html)

- Date Published: 2026-09-21
- Date of effect: Unknown
- Date last modified: 2026-09-21

Replaces the 2024 Cabinet Directive on Regulatory and Permitting Efficiency for Clean Growth Projects. Sets expectations for federal project decisions within one year of a comprehensive application and required information, subject to the directive's scope and exceptions. The page does not separately state an effective date.

## [Cabinet Directive on Regulation (CDR)](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/laws/developing-improving-federal-regulations/requirements-developing-managing-reviewing-regulations/cabinet-directive-regulation.html)

- Status: In Force
- Date Published: 2018-06-28
- Date of effect: 2018-09-01
- Date last modified: 2026-10-21

Sets expectations for developing, managing, and reviewing federal regulations. Section 9.0 establishes the effective date. The source announces replacement by the Cabinet Directive on Regulatory Competitiveness and Red Tape Reduction on **2026-12-31**; this is a future replacement, so CDR remains In Force as of 2026-10-22.

Replacement reported: [Cabinet Directive on Regulatory Competitiveness and Red Tape Reduction](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/laws/developing-improving-federal-regulations/red-tape-reduction-office/regulatory-reform-reduce-red-tape/cabinet-directive-regulatory-competitiveness-red-tape-reduction.html). Source assessment: announced.

## [Cabinet Directive on the Coordinated Response to Threats to Elections (CRTE)](https://www.canada.ca/en/democratic-institutions/services/protecting-democracy/coordinated-response-threats-elections/cabinet.html)

- Status: In Force
- Date Published: 2026-03-26
- Date of effect: Unknown
- Date last modified: 2026-03-27

Sets a coordinated response to threats to federal general elections. Its incident-response mandate applies during caretaker periods; the directive also sets preparatory activities outside those periods. That limited application is distinct from the status of the instrument itself.
