# Amendments to the Directive on the Management of Real Property – Collection of Public Purpose Interests

Date: September 21, 2026

## Overview

Effective September 21, 2026, subsection F.2.2 of the Directive on the Management of Real Property has been amended.

Departments now have greater flexibility in how they collect expressions of public purpose interest when disposing of federal real property. Rather than having to seek interest from all defined groups at the same time, departments can seek interest simultaneously or sequentially from other federal departments, agent Crown corporations, provinces, municipalities and Indigenous groups.

The decision to proceed simultaneously or sequentially falls within the accountability of the department and should reflect the circumstances and characteristics of the transaction. Where a department chooses to collect interests sequentially, it must follow the order of precedence for public purpose disposals established in subsection F.2.3 of the Directive.

Departments remain responsible for completing all required due diligence and maintaining adequate documentation of the rationale for why it chose to collect interests sequentially or simultaneously.
