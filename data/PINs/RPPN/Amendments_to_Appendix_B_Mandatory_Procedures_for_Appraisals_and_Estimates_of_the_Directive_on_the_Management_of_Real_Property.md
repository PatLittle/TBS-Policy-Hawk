# Real Property Policy Notice 2026-2: Amendments to Appendix B: Mandatory Procedures for Appraisals and Estimates of the Directive on the Management of Real Property

Date: July 24, 2026

## Overview

Effective July 24, 2026, Appendix B: Mandatory Procedures for Appraisals and Estimates of the Directive on the Management of Real Property was amended as follows:

1. The threshold above which a formal appraisal by the Chief Appraiser of Canada is required for non-competitive leases and licences (subsection B.2.2.1.2) has been expanded to include non-competitive transactions of all types (for example, purchases, leases, licences, sales).
2. The threshold above which a formal appraisal by the Chief Appraiser of Canada is required for non-competitive real property transactions of all types has been increased from $25,000 to $100,000.

| Transaction type | Before amendment | After amendment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open solicitation (competitive) | $500,000 | $500,000 |
| No open solicitation (non-competitive) | $0 | $100,000 |

| Transaction type | Before amendment | After amendment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open solicitation (competitive) | $500,000 | $500,000 |
| No open solicitation (non-competitive) | $25,000 | $100,000 |

This change supports more efficient disposals of surplus federal real property and optimizes the use of appraisal resources relative to transaction value and complexity.

For in-flight transactions, departments should determine the appropriate application of the amended requirements based on status and stage of approval as of July 24, 2026.

Appraisals support departmental accountability and are the basis upon which a market value justification should be made. They are a key source of information supporting real property transactions.
